Open Crossroads · frontend review

Six models. Six ways into a room.

Independent interface directions for a self-sorting research-conference lobby: inspect who is talking, approach a consented doorway, listen to a synthetic conversation pulse, then choose whether to enter the Google Meet.

Synthetic people and conversations No real Meet links Desktop + mobile Public review page

How to review

Try the attendance switcher, inspect several rooms, use “Take me somewhere,” enter a doorway, and test the Meet handoff. Open a draft full-screen for a fair mobile test. Use the comment button at bottom-right to leave feedback on this page.

  1. Can you tell where the good conversations are?
  2. Does approaching feel low-risk and socially legible?
  3. Would you understand what “listening” means?
  4. Does the interface help rather than gamify?
