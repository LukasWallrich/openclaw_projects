Why it wins

  • Highest cliffs and deepest valley in the set.
  • Baie-Saint-Paul supplies the genuinely cute town.
  • Strong rain fallback: valley floor, food, galleries.

Main-hike essentials

  • September Acropole starts must be before noon; summit descent by 15:30.
  • It is hard and 5–10°C colder at the summit.
  • The 27 km / 30 min approach is from Saint-Aimé-des-Lacs to the visitor centre, not after the gate.

Acropole-des-Draveurs · field guide

The day to prepare for

11.2 km return800 m ascentOfficial time: 4–6 hoursDifficult

The trail starts at Le Pin-Blanc campground, park-road km 6, climbs through forest and reaches an alpine summit area. Budget 6–7 hours including breaks as a conservative planning allowance, rather than racing the official estimate. Official trail table ↗

What the climb involves

The official elevation profile runs from approximately 250 m to three summits: 920 m, 1,020 m and 1,048 m. The first summit is not the highest. Assess energy, weather and time before continuing; returning early is a valid plan.

This is a sustained uphill-and-downhill effort, not a casual viewpoint stroll. Both of you should be comfortable with several hours on uneven ground and a long descent.

September cutoffs & conditions

Latest permitted start: 12:00. Mandatory departure from summit 3: 15:30. Treat these as limits, not targets. No dogs on this trail.

For the view, avoid a summit buried in cloud. For footing and exposure, reconsider in heavy rain, thunderstorms, ice or winds that affect balance. Ask staff about actual conditions; town sunshine is not a mountain all-clear.

Sépaq’s hike rules ↗

Getting to the start

Via Route 138 and Saint-Aimé-des-Lacs: 27 km / about 30 min to Félix-Antoine-Savard visitor centre. From La Malbaie it is 48 km to that centre; allow about an hour to the trailhead, plus parking/check-in buffer.

The published shuttle runs weekends/holidays only in late September (08:00–18:00; last Draveur departure 17:30). No scheduled shuttle Monday/Tuesday: plan to drive to designated Pin-Blanc trailhead parking, but confirm road/parking instructions with staff. Don’t plan a one-way valley walk relying on a bus.

A realistic hike-day rhythm

  1. ~07:00: leave La Malbaie after breakfast, with lunch and water already packed.
  2. ~08:00: visitor-centre check. It opens at 08:00 on your dates.
  3. 08:30–09:00: start walking from Pin-Blanc.
  4. Late morning / early afternoon: summit area if your pace and conditions allow; keep breaks flexible.
  5. ~13:00–13:30: suggested descent target, earlier if needed; don’t wait for the official closure.
  6. ~15:00–16:00: intended finish, with buffer. Avoid a fixed early dinner reservation.

These are planning targets, not guaranteed timings. Turn around if your pace, comfort or weather no longer fits them.

Pack & download before leaving

  • At least 2 litres of drinking water each, plus lunch and snacks. Untreated streams are not drinking-water sources.
  • Grippy hiking footwear and trekking poles; take particular care descending.
  • Warm layer, waterproof/windproof shell, hat and gloves for stops.
  • Headlamp, charged phone, power bank, basic first-aid/blister kit and emergency layer.
  • Offline map and saved entry proof. Don’t rely on mobile coverage in the park; tell someone your route and intended return.

Dry toilets: trailhead and km 3. Fill water at the visitor centres. Official preparation advice ↗

If the summit is not the right call

Keep the valley, lose the pressure.

Low cloud, but reasonable walking weather

Start at Draveur with Le Belvédère (0.4 km return, 55 m ascent, about 15 min) and Le Pied-des-Sommets (0.5 km loop, about 15 min). Add an out-and-back section of Les Rapides to suit conditions.

Watch the units: Les Rapides is 7.6 km one way, not return. With no weekday shuttle, retrace your steps rather than expecting a lift. Trail options ↗

Rain, recovery, or simply a slower day

Keep Baie-Saint-Paul’s galleries and cafés, the La Malbaie waterfront and scenic Route 362 as flexible options. Check individual opening hours, especially Monday. Don’t replace an unsafe summit with a long wet drive or an exposed Grands-Jardins summit.

The park’s current notice lists river cruises at 11:00, 13:00 and 14:45 and equipment rentals 09:00–17:00. Confirm the exact date, weather operation and cruise availability before relying on them; park entry and an activity booking are separate. Service notice ↗
