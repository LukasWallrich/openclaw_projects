What this prototype proves

Real now: adaptive room rules, occupancy cues, routing, responsive interaction, keyboard control, and the Meet handoff journey.

Synthetic for now: the other attendees and room counts. A production version would replace the in-page scenario data with ephemeral presence from Supabase Realtime and gate the room URLs behind attendee access.

Deliberately absent: video, permanent profiles, tracking, and organiser-heavy matchmaking.
